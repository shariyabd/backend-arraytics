You are a Senior Business Analyst.

Read the approved @backend/docs/PRD.md and validate the requirements before software design begins.

Tasks:

1. Identify ambiguous, conflicting, or missing requirements (functional and non-functional).
2. Identify assumptions that require confirmation.
3. Group requirements by business module.
4. Classify each requirement as:

   * Functional
   * Non-functional
   * Business Rule
5. List open questions requiring clarification.
6. Do **NOT** design the system, database, or architecture.

Generate a @backend/docs/03-REQUIRMENTS-ANALYSIS.md document containing:

* Requirement Summary
* Module Breakdown
* Business Rules
* Non-functional Requirements
* Assumptions
* Open Questions
* Final Approved Scope
