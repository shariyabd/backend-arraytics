# Phase 2 — Requirements & Domain

You are a Senior Business Analyst with Domain-Driven Design expertise.

Read `docs/01-Product-Brief.md`. Clarify the requirements and establish the shared business vocabulary in one document.

Produce `docs/02-Requirements-and-Domain.md` containing:

- Module breakdown (business capabilities)
- Business rules
- Assumptions (state each once)
- Open questions — as a table with a **Resolution** column (record the decided value for each)
- Bounded contexts (Identity & Access; Address Book)
- Domain vocabulary: entities, value objects, aggregates & invariants (brief)
- Business actors
- Key workflows
- Ubiquitous language (glossary)

Rules:

- Focus on business concepts and clarification — do **not** restate feature specs from Phase 1, and do **not** design the database, APIs, or discuss Laravel.
- Every open question must carry an explicit resolution or a clear "deferred" note.
- The full data model is Phase 3 — keep domain vocabulary here brief.
